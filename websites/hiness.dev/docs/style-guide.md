# Design direction · paper theatre (`hiness.dev` & `hiness.app`)

**Reference:** The site keeps the clean page/data/layout split from the Parlant Jekyll template while embracing the chosen domains **`hiness.dev`** (CLI & OTP daemon) and **`hiness.app`** (`H.app` & `hiweb` workbench) with a cool, ink-and-paper theatre aesthetic. From H itself, keep the strict structural grammar: an entry is a point, a continuation is a line, and a fork produces two labeled paths. Never draw a client as a branch or a fork as a second worker.

## Visual and interaction decisions

- **Ink, periwinkle, and chalk:** The two sites import `websites/shared/assets/css/palette.css`, with navy `#101422` for the page, periwinkle `#b8c7ff` for accents, chalk `#f5f7ff` for text, and a separate green for running status. Both sites use the geometric H mark, with a heavier, high-contrast version for favicons and touch icons. No runtime web-font, analytics, or CDN requests.
- **Typography:** Headings, body copy, and controls use system sans-serif. Commands, paths, and labels use monospace. System serif is reserved for italic display emphasis and house mottoes. Prose stays near 65-70 characters per line.
- **Paper stage:** Both home pages use the shared `ballroom.html` and `theatre.css`. A cool-toned crop of a Luigi Premazzi watercolor fills the viewport width. Crisp WebP paper cutouts retain the original pigments with brighter midtones and clearer details. A wooden rod rises from below the stage and attaches behind each cutout. Figures rock on their rods as they walk, turn to face their direction of travel, and take staggered routes out of and back into the ballroom. The dance pauses when the stage leaves view or the tab is hidden. Reduced motion and no-script views stay still. The cast is smaller on mobile.
- **Artwork and credits:** Portrait panels use the same cutouts without plaques. The footer links to `/artwork/` on each site for the original images, museum/source records, and rights information specific to each work and reproduction. Artwork metadata lives in `websites/shared/data/artworks.yml`; cutout sizes and cast order live in `websites/shared/data/ballroom.yml`.
- **Royal Houses = Harness Profiles (`_data/houses.yml`, `_includes/sections/houses.html`):** Cards share row tracks for portraits, text, metadata, and links. They use four columns on wide screens, two on tablets, and one on narrow screens. Four royal houses map truthfully to `H.Harness` session profiles:
  1. **House of Habsburg** -> Built-in `coding` (`H.Harness.Coding`: `read`, `write`, `edit`, `bash`, `search_history`, `subagent`, hierarchical `AGENTS.md`).
  2. **House of Bonaparte** -> Starter extension template (`example.greeting.v1` via `H.Extension` + `H.Harness`).
  3. **House of Stuart** -> Custom read-only/steward `@behaviour H.Harness` profile.
  4. **House of Wittelsbach** -> Embedded `:harness` OTP application profile + custom `H.ExecutionEnv`.
- **Walkthroughs and disclosures:** Native `<details>` disclosures and pure-CSS radio tabs drive the installation selector, architecture lifetimes figure, and client demos without hydration or external scripts.
- **Accessibility and verification:** Wide layouts pair the introduction with its product specimen above the full-width ballroom; narrow viewports show the ballroom before the specimen. Maintain one `<h1>` per page, a visible skip link, WCAG AA dark-surface contrast, keyboard focus outlines, straight quotes only, zero em dashes, and `make verify` compliance.
